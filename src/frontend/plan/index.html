MeetPoint

Find a meeting place that's fair for everyone in your group to reach, in any city.

How it works

  1. Name your meetup and choose how people will travel: car, two-wheeler, public transport, walking or cycling.
  2. Add where everyone is starting from, or share the invite link so friends add themselves.
  3. Get five venues ranked by fairness, with each person's travel time side by side.

Why it's fair

MeetPoint doesn't just pick the midpoint on a map. It compares real travel times for every person and prefers places where the longest trip is short and trips are even, so the friend who lives farthest away isn't always the one travelling an hour.
