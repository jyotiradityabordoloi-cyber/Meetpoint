MeetPoint shortens the longest trip

A place that is 5 minutes for one friend and 50 for another loses to one that is 25 for both. Every spot gets one score:

  1. Half the longest trip anyone makes
  2. Three tenths how uneven the trips are
  3. One fifth the average trip

Does MeetPoint cost anything?

No. It is free, and nobody in the group needs an account.

How does MeetPoint work out travel times?

From road distance and typical speeds for each way of travelling. Live traffic is not counted yet, so a rush-hour trip can run longer.

Can each person in the group travel differently?

Yes. Set car, two-wheeler, transit, bike or walking for the group, then change it for anyone.

Where does MeetPoint get ratings and photos?

Each place links to its own Google Maps page, where its rating, photos and reviews are.

Who can see the group's starting points?

Only people with the meetup link. Meetup pages are kept out of search engines and deleted after 30 days.
